Colds in Children: A Phoenix Parent's Guide

The common cold is a frequent visitor in households, especially in a dry desert climate like Phoenix. The common cold is a mild infection of your upper respiratory tract (which includes your nose and throat). More than 200 different viruses can cause a cold, but rhinoviruses are the most common type . Understanding how to manage your child's cold and when to seek help is crucial.
