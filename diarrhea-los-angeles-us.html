Diarrhea in Children: A Los Angeles Parent's Guide

Diarrhea is a common illness in children. It is defined as having three or more loose, watery stools in a single day (Source: NIH). While most cases are mild and short-lived, diarrhea can lead to serious dehydration if not managed properly. Rotavirus is the most common cause of acute diarrhea in children, and the infection often causes vomiting as well (Source: NIH).
