Fever in a 3-Year-Old: Recognizing Emergency Signs

Fever is a common sign that your child&#x27;s body is fighting an infection, and most cases are harmless and resolve on their own. You can learn what the common warning signs are (Source: NIH | Toddler Health). However, it can be difficult to know when a fever signals something more serious. This guide will help you recognize the emergency signs that require immediate medical attention for your 3-year-old.
