Stomach Bug in a 4-Year-Old: Home Care and When to Seek Help

Stomach bugs (gastroenteritis) are common in young children. The illness is typically caused by viruses and leads to vomiting and diarrhea. While seeing your child sick is concerning, most cases can be managed at home. The most important goal is to prevent dehydration, which happens when a child loses more fluid than they take in.
