Pink Eye in Children: A Chicago Parent's Guide

Pink eye, also known as conjunctivitis, is one of the most common eye conditions in children . It refers to an inflammation of the conjunctiva, the thin, transparent layer of tissue that covers the white part of the eye and lines the inner eyelid . The condition causes the blood vessels in the eye to swell, leading to the characteristic pink or red appearance .
