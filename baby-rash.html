Baby Pink Eye: What Parents Need to Know

Pink eye (conjunctivitis) in babies is common and can be caused by infections, irritants, or blocked tear ducts. While most cases are mild, knowing when to seek medical attention is essential.

What Is Pink Eye?

Pink eye is an inflammation of the conjunctiva—the thin, clear tissue that covers the white part of the eye and lines the inside of the eyelid. It can cause redness, swelling, discharge, and discomfort.

Causes of Pink Eye in Babies

Pink eye in babies can be caused by:

Viral infections (often associated with colds)

Bacterial infections (can cause thick, yellow-green discharge)

Irritants (such as shampoo, dust, or smoke)

Blocked tear ducts (common in newborns, causing watery eyes and mild discharge)

Minor eye irritations, such as shampoo or dust in the eye, often get better on their own within a day (Source: NHS | Eye injuries – Eye injuries). However, persistent redness or discharge requires evaluation.
