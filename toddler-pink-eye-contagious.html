What Parents Should Know

While the sources do not provide specific guidance, the most important takeaway for parents is:

Pink eye (conjunctivitis) can be caused by viruses, bacteria, or allergies. Viral and bacterial conjunctivitis are highly contagious and can spread easily among children in daycare and preschool settings.

General Guidelines (Not from Sources)

Viral conjunctivitis – highly contagious; often associated with cold symptoms

Bacterial conjunctivitis – highly contagious; often with thick, yellow-green discharge

Allergic conjunctivitis – NOT contagious; caused by allergens like pollen or pet dander

When to seek medical help:

Eye redness with thick discharge

Eye pain or sensitivity to light

Fever with eye symptoms

Symptoms that do not improve after a few days

Your child has a weakened immune system

Prevention (Not from Sources)

Wash hands frequently

Avoid sharing towels, pillowcases, or eye drops

Keep your child home from daycare or school until cleared by a doctor

Trust your instincts. If you are worried about your toddler, call your health care provider right away.
