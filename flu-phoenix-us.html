Flu in Children: A Phoenix Parent's Guide

The flu is a serious respiratory illness caused by the influenza virus, and it can affect children of all ages . In Phoenix, the flu season can be unpredictable, and it's crucial for parents to be prepared. While a common cold is a mild infection of the upper respiratory tract, the flu can cause much more severe symptoms and lead to serious complications, especially in young children .
