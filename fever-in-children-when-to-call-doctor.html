Fever in Children: When to Call the Doctor

Fever is a common sign that your child's body is fighting an infection, and it is usually a harmless part of recovery . While most fevers pass on their own, there are specific guidelines to help you decide when to contact your pediatrician . If you are ever worried about your child, you should call your health care provider right away .

What is a Fever?

A fever is defined as a temporary increase in the body's temperature . A child is considered to have a fever at temperatures of 38.5°C (101.3°F) or more in children, and at 38.0°C (100.4°F) or more in babies under three months old . For infants, the most accurate measurement is a rectal temperature .

When to Call the Doctor

You should contact your child's healthcare provider if your child has a fever and:

Age-specific guidelines:

Under 3 months old: Any temperature of 38.0°C (100.4°F) or higher requires immediate medical attention .

3 to 12 months old: A fever of 39.0°C (102.2°F) or higher .

Fever duration:

For a child 2 years or younger, a fever lasting more than 24 to 48 hours .

For an older child, a fever for longer than 48 to 72 hours .

Any fever that lasts more than 3 days .

Other concerning symptoms:

Fever with a stiff neck, which can be a sign of meningitis .

A new rash or bruising . A skin rash that doesn't fade if you press against it is a particularly important warning sign .

Difficulty breathing or breathing very quickly .

Signs of dehydration, such as no wet diapers for 8 hours, a dry mouth, or no tears when crying .

The child is very sleepy, unresponsive, or in a lot of pain .

Underlying conditions: If your child has a serious medical illness, such as a heart problem, diabetes, or a weakened immune system .

When to Seek Emergency Care

Call 911 or go to the emergency room if your child has a fever and:

Cannot be awakened easily or is confused .

Has blue lips, tongue, or nails .

Has a seizure .

Has a very bad headache .

Trust your instincts. Parents are often the best judges of whether their child is behaving abnormally . If you are concerned, it is always best to seek medical advice.
