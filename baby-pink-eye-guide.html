Baby Pink Eye in Children 0: Complete Parent Guide

Pink eye (conjunctivitis) is one of the most common eye conditions in babies and young children . It can be alarming for parents, but the vast majority of cases are not serious and will resolve on their own. This guide will help you understand the causes, symptoms, and when to seek medical care.

What Is Pink Eye?

Pink eye is an inflammation of the conjunctiva—the thin, clear tissue that covers the white part of the eye and lines the inside of the eyelid . It can affect one or both eyes and is a common condition in young children .

Types of Pink Eye

1. Viral Conjunctivitis

Cause: Most commonly caused by adenoviruses, often associated with colds

Characteristics: Watery discharge, usually starts in one eye and may spread to the other

Treatment: Resolves on its own within 1weeks

Spread: Highly contagious
