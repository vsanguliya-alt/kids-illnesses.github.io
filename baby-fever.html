## Frequently Asked Questions **When should I call a doctor about fever?** If your child has difficulty breathing, persistent high fever, lethargy, or symptoms lasting beyond expected, contact a healthcare provider. **Is fever normal at this age?** Many variations are normal. Red-flag signs (listed above) mean you should seek care. **What helps at home?** Hydration, rest, and age-appropriate comfort measures. Always follow NHS/AAP guidance for medications. **How long does it last?** Most childhood episodes resolve within a few days. Persistent or worsening symptoms need evaluation.
