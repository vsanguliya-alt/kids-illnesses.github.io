Flu in Children: A Houston Parent's Guide

Flu season is a serious public health concern in Houston, and children are among the most vulnerable. The flu is a respiratory illness caused by the influenza virus, which affects the whole body . Unlike a common cold, the flu comes on suddenly and can be far more severe .

Recognizing the Symptoms

Flu symptoms in children include a high fever (often 103°F to 105°F), severe body aches and headache, cough, sore throat, runny or stuffy nose, and extreme fatigue that can last for weeks . Children

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I call a doctor about flu?

If your child has difficulty breathing, persistent high fever, lethargy, or symptoms lasting beyond expected, contact a healthcare provider.

Is flu normal at this age?

Many variations are normal. Red-flag signs (listed above) mean you should seek care.

What helps at home?

Hydration, rest, and age-appropriate comfort measures. Always follow NHS/AAP guidance for medications.

How long does it last?

Most childhood episodes resolve within a few days. Persistent or worsening symptoms need evaluation.
