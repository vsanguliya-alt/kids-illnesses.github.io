Flu in Children: A Houston Parent's Guide

Flu season is a serious public health concern in Houston, and children are among the most vulnerable. The flu is a respiratory illness caused by the influenza virus, which affects the whole body . Unlike a common cold, the flu comes on suddenly and can be far more severe .

Recognizing the Symptoms

Flu symptoms in children include a high fever (often 103°F to 105°F), severe body aches and headache, cough, sore throat, runny or stuffy nose, and extreme fatigue that can last for weeks . Children
